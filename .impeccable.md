## Design Context

### Users

anime is for viewers who want one place to search, manage, and watch anime,
series, and movies from user-managed video sources. They may use a desktop for
search-heavy browsing and a phone or tablet for touch-first viewing.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and content-first. The interface should feel like a dependable
media tool rather than a marketing page.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a restrained dark viewing environment with warm poster surfaces and crisp
controls. Navigation should stay quiet; covers, source health, and playback
state should carry the visual weight.

### Design Principles

- Put usable actions on the first screen: browse, search, continue watching,
  and source status.
- Treat desktop and mobile as different contexts: side navigation and dense
  panels on desktop, bottom navigation and large touch targets on mobile.
- Keep source risk visible without blocking exploration.
- Prefer direct, readable Chinese UI copy.
- Use motion and visual emphasis only for state changes and playback context.
